## 💡 Key Concepts Explained

### Supplier Display Issue
**Problem**: Shows "To be assigned" always  
**Solution**: Join with offers table, use supplier_name from offers  
**Documents**: 
- Details: BONS_COMMANDES_CARD_DISPLAY_FIX.md (Problem 1 section)
- Visual: BON_CARD_VISUAL_COMPARISON.md (Problem 1 section)
- Code: BON_CARD_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md (Feature 1 section)

### Total Amount Display
**Problem**: Shows "0 DA" confusingly when no products  
**Solution**: Display "Pending" when 0, amount when > 0  
**Documents**:
- Details: BONS_COMMANDES_CARD_DISPLAY_FIX.md (Problem 3 section)
- Visual: BON_CARD_VISUAL_COMPARISON.md (Problem 2 section)
- Code: BON_CARD_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md (Feature 2 section)

### Product Count Indicator
**Problem**: No indication if products were added  
**Solution**: Track and display product count on card  
**Documents**:
- Details: BONS_COMMANDES_CARD_DISPLAY_FIX.md (Problem 4 section)
- Visual: BON_CARD_VISUAL_COMPARISON.md (Problem 3 section)
- Code: BON_CARD_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md (Feature 3 section)

### Card Layout
**Problem**: Cramped, hard to read format  
**Solution**: Redesign with sections, colors, hierarchy  
**Documents**:
- Details: BONS_COMMANDES_CARD_DISPLAY_FIX.md (Problem 1 section)
- Visual: BON_CARD_VISUAL_COMPARISON.md (Full comparison)
- Code: BON_CARD_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md (Feature 4 section)
